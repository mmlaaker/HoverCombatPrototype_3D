Build Your Own Hover Racers
You can build your own hover racers by quickly modifying the modular examples located in each scene in the collection folder. In addition, you can also assemble your own hover racer from scratch by opening up the modules scene and moving/rotating/scaling and duplicating the modules until you have something you are happy with.
----------------------------------------

Changing Colors
If the included colors in this pack are not enough, you can feel free to use the PSD/Masks files in the masks folders to create your own colors using any photo editing software.
